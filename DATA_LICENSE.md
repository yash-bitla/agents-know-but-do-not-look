# Data license

The labels and derived data in `data/`, `labelling/`, `round2/` and `bench/` (incident and cause labels, mechanism codes,
counts, model predictions, sandbox results) are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Issue titles, URLs and short quotes come from public GitHub issues. They remain the property of their authors and are
included only to identify each report. Issue bodies are not redistributed.
